# Maxionlabs SEO / AEO strategy: fundamentals (approved by Max 2026-11-15, MAX-738)

Research inputs (all 2026-11-14/15): live-site audit, 20-query search landscape, buyer calls + emails (Cosmo, Heritage, Keith, Unruh), 20 held-call non-buyers, `industry-voice-2026-11-14.md`, Strong Tower page (`strongtower-offer.maxionlabs.workers.dev`).

**Revenue path:** speculative. Inbound adds to cold email, never replaces it. Near-term payoff is indirect: every cold prospect checks the site before paying, and the site currently fails that check.

---

## 1. Who we attract (ICP)

- **Company:** US independent commercial P&C or benefits agency/brokerage, ~$2M-15M revenue.
- **Person:** owner, CEO, principal or president (COO or VP BD only with the decider booked).
- **The defining trait:** at least 2 producers besides the owner, and nobody owns prospecting. Someone other than the buyer takes the meetings. (4 of 4 buyers; 7 of 20 non-buyers were the only seller.)
- **Qualified by** (Max, 2026-11-15): premium size, renewal date, industry. That is their own language: buyers qualify in premium ($25K+, $50K+ floors), not headcount; Wienckoski and Gabbel heard headcount as "you don't know my business".
- **Trigger:** a renewal window arriving with nobody prospecting (Jan 1 Heritage, January Cosmo, Feb WC Unruh).

## 2. Who we repel (anti-ICP)

Solo agents · in-person-only shops · personal-lines-led agencies · captives · benefits sellers approached Oct-Dec (AEP) · anyone asking for free samples or pay-on-close in the first 10 minutes (0 of 8 bought) · non-owners with no decider booked (0 of 4 advanced) · other lead-gen agencies and DIY marketers reading for tips.

The site should say who it is NOT for, in plain words. About 9 of the 20 losses were never our buyer.

## 3. Positioning

**Max's instinct is confirmed by the research:** owners' burn words are "leads", "telemarketer", "appointment setting", "lead company" (industry-voice 6f). The words they use for what they want: "outbound seat", "SDR", "force multiplier", "feed them leads" (inside sales), "first meetings with decision makers".

- **Category, on the page:** your missing outbound seat. First meetings with owners of the businesses you already write, timed to their renewal.
- **Promise, in buyers' words:** "If you just get us in the room, that's where we're good" (Heritage). We get them in the room; their producers close.
- **Comparison:** hiring a producer (52-57% validate, ~3 years; Reagan/IIABA). Used on the sales call and in an ROI section, not as the headline (no buyer framed it that way unprompted).
- **Keywords stay commodity:** pages still rank for "commercial insurance leads" and "appointment setting for insurance agencies", because that is what they type. The page reframes once they land.

## 5. Where to appear

1. **Our own site** (Google + AI crawlers). Today: zero indexed pages, no sitemap/robots/llms.txt/schema, offer page orphaned.
2. **AI answers** (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity) for the solution and comparison questions. Only real competitor there is Abstrakt (generic B2B, no insurance case study, already optimising for AI citations).
3. **Third-party pages AI cites for these questions:** Insurance Journal (incl. its forum thread on what a set commercial appointment is worth), IA Magazine / Big I, Agency Performance Partners blog + podcast, insurance-forums.com, Power Producers and Agency Freedom podcasts, The Digital/Modern Insurance Agent podcasts. Clutch/G2 profile for the comparison queries.
4. **Not Reddit:** zero Reddit URLs across 20 queries; also Max's standing no on Reddit as a channel.

## 6. Order of work

**Phase 0, now (hazards, already costing deals):** `/roi` shows retired step-down tier and +1,400 territory lock; `/insurance` promises cross-sell/win-back (off the page since 2026-10-24); homepage sells pay-per-held across three verticals (footers checked 2026-11-15: both say Ukraine, no conflict); old landscaping and workers.dev pages public.

**Phase 1, trust site (for cold prospects already visiting):** homepage becomes the insurance offer; pages for how it works (trigger sources, the check call, the brief), what qualified means, guarantee in writing, who we are (named entity, Stripe, who runs the calls), how sending domains work, who it is not for. Strong Tower page is the template: it already holds mechanism, market sizing, sample copy, the 15 owner replies and a real brief.

**Phase 2, proof:** Cosmo and Heritage results need a `QUOTABLE` row in `proof/funnel-tracker.md` before the site quotes them (none today). Then a named quote or short video from Connie or Jeremy, which is also the AEO asset: a third-party-verifiable insurance result.

**Phase 3, pages built to rank and be cited** (no one answers these for a commercial owner today):
1. What a qualified commercial appointment is worth, and what it should cost.
2. Are commercial insurance leads worth it: shared leads vs referrals vs booked first meetings, judged on cost per bound account.
3. Why your agency stopped growing: producer capacity vs no outbound vs book mix.
4. Line x state market pages from public data (e.g. "new trucking authorities in Arizona this month"), linked from the site, each one written for a human (no orphan programmatic pages).

**Phase 4, citations:** pitch the recurring pages and podcasts in section 5 with the Phase 3 data; client video descriptions and directory listings that say "commercial insurance".

## 7. How we measure

- The one that counts: "how did you find us?" on every inbound call, logged in Fluid. Net profit from deals sourced, not traffic.
- Leading: Google Search Console impressions/indexed pages; monthly AI-visibility run of the 20 buyer questions (Daydream's free dashboard or our own script), counting whether we are cited.

## 8. Non-goals and anti-goals

No blog spam, no orphan programmatic pages, no Reddit astroturf, no black-hat tactics, no AI-written filler. Fail state: traffic from marketers and solo agents, or a page that quotes a number we cannot defend.

## 9. Decisions (Max, 2026-11-15)

1. **Price on the page: publish it.** Supersedes the "no price on the page" line in `brand/landing-copy-live.md`.
2. **The $500 deposit stays off the site.** Max finds it hard to say on a call; it is not a standard term (`offer-v3.md` unchanged).
3. **Homepage = commercial insurance.** Landscaping and cost segregation each get their own page, linked but not on the homepage.
4. **Max's time: about 2 hours a week** (approvals, one client quote or video). Agents draft the rest.

## 10. Content formats

- **Answer pages, not a blog cadence.** 4-6 deep pages (Phase 3), each answering one buyer question better than what AI cites today, updated quarterly. More only when one of them gets cited or brings a call.
- **Public data dashboard (the asymmetric bet).** A free, public "who just changed" tracker for commercial agencies: new trucking authorities, contractor licences, liquor licences by state, refreshed monthly from the public sources we already pull. It is the thing publishers and AI answers cite, it demos the mechanism, and nobody in the category has one. Cost: data we already build for campaigns.
- **Our own AI-visibility tracker.** Run the 20 buyer questions monthly (Daydream's free dashboard first; a script if we outgrow it) and log whether we are cited and who is.

## 11. Taken from the shared Slack material

- **Matt White:** search the questions you want to rank for, see who the AI cites, write that style of page better. llms.txt and FAQs on every page. His own numbers are the caution: 2.2K people and 6.7K AI fetches in 28 days produced 7 signups. Traffic is not the goal.
- **Ben (Apero):** content a human would want to read; orphan programmatic pages lose authority; YouTube and third-party mentions drive AI authority most. Hence client videos and Phase 4.
- **Eric D:** press releases and Reddit tactics worked in 2025 and faded in late 2026. Skip both.
- **Daydream method:** pitch only pages AI cites repeatedly across questions and runs, check referring domains, and first ask the AI what you cost: if it cites a stale pricing page, fix that before pitching anyone. Our `/roi` is exactly that stale page (Phase 0).
- **Tools (AirOps, Jam, Sunbeam, Catalyst):** not now. Nothing here needs a paid platform until Phase 3 pages exist.
